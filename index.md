## In one sentence

SAVE20 links a new OKX account to an affiliate rate of 20%, which is returned to you from the fees on qualifying spot and perpetual order-book trades.

## Getting it right at registration

The code can only be applied while the account is being created. Following [okx.com/join/SAVE20](https://www.okx.com/join/SAVE20) fills it in for you; otherwise type SAVE20 into the referral box yourself. Skip that step and the account can never have a code attached.

## What earns the rebate

- Limit and market orders on the spot order book
- Perpetual contract trades on the order book

What doesn't: the Buy and Convert features, which use a quoted price instead of the order book.

## Payment timing

Your trade is charged at the regular rate first. The rebated share arrives as a separate credit later on.

## Identity checks

Referral rewards are only paid to accounts that complete OKX's identity verification.

## For new users in the EEA

The sign-up page advertised new-user tasks worth "up to €400" when we looked. Roughly a quarter of that figure is a €100 X-Perps voucher; the remainder is cash released in stages as your trading volume grows.

## Country limitations

Spain is excluded from the rebate. The Netherlands and Malta receive it for spot trading only. US residents can't use this offer, nor can people in restricted jurisdictions such as Canada, India, Japan, Hong Kong and Malaysia.
